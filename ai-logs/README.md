# AI logs

This folder is how you show us your work **with** the AI. It is required.

- **[`PROMPTS.md`](./PROMPTS.md)** — a running log of the meaningful prompts you sent the AI,
  in order, each with a one-line note on why and what you did with the result. Keep it as you
  go; do not reconstruct it at the end.
- **[`exports/`](./exports)** — at least one **raw exported chat** from your AI tool. This is
  the full transcript, not a summary. See **[`HOW-TO-EXPORT.md`](./HOW-TO-EXPORT.md)**.
- **[`DECISIONS.md`](./DECISIONS.md)** — optional but appreciated: short notes on decisions you
  made (especially where you and the AI disagreed).

We are not judging you for "using AI a lot". We are judging whether you **drive** it: clear
prompts, catching its mistakes, rejecting slop, owning the result.
