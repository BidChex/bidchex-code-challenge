# AI logs

This folder is how you show us your work **with** the AI. It is required.

- **[`exports/`](./exports)** - **required**: at least one **raw exported chat** from your AI
  tool (the full transcript, not a summary). See **[`HOW-TO-EXPORT.md`](./HOW-TO-EXPORT.md)**.
  This is the main artifact we read.
- **[`PROMPTS.md`](./PROMPTS.md)** - a running log of the prompts you sent the AI.
  **Optional if your chat export already captures them.** Use it when you cannot export a full
  transcript, or to call out the key prompts.
- **[`DECISIONS.md`](./DECISIONS.md)** - optional but appreciated: short notes on decisions you
  made (especially where you and the AI disagreed).

We are not judging you for "using AI a lot". We are judging whether you **drive** it: clear
prompts, catching its mistakes, rejecting slop, owning the result.
